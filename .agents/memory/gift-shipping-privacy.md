name: Gift shipping privacy
description: Privacy boundaries and retailer-delivery assumptions for future gift work.
---

Preserve recipient ownership of legacy delivery records without making those records available to payers, even after payment. A recipient confirms private delivery directly with the retailer; PayGate must not become a shipping operator or hold product funds.

**Why:** The platform charges a service fee, not the product price. Recognizing an approved retailer or selecting Amazon's ordinary gift checkbox does not establish that checkout hides an address. Privacy guidance must distinguish recipient attestation from independent retailer verification and tell users to stop when private delivery is unavailable.

**How to apply:** Keep buyer privacy independent of gift/payment status, preserve recipient-only legacy access, and maintain truthful retailer and service-fee copy. Keep release verification free of automatic notification side effects without changing normal product messaging. Follow [the GitHub backup rule](github-backup.md): verify the published green state before backup; use only the existing Replit GitHub link and stop with AUTH BLOCK on Git authentication failure.

Checkpoint identifiers and verification results belong in Git history and the delivery report, not this durable note.
