## P0 — Fix first

### 1. Chapter payments are one-click with NO confirmation — caused a real $5 charge during QA
- While inspecting the QA Alice pending match card on /matches, the "Pay $5 to
  Advance" button SUBMITTED IMMEDIATELY on click — no confirmation dialog.
- Result: $5 deducted from the wallet ($15.00 → $10.00; wallet history shows
  "Gate 1: Payment −$5.00"), match moved from Pending to Active at Chapter 2
  (toast: "Chapter Advanced! You've turned the page. Keep the story going!").
- This is a product defect, not just a QA accident: the FAQ stresses fees are
  non-refundable ("Only deposit what you plan to use"), yet money moves on a
  single click with no "are you sure" step.
- Fix: add a confirmation dialog to every chapter payment showing amount, gate,
  and resulting balance, requiring explicit confirm. Consider also an undo
  window or support-assisted reversal path.
- Peter's decision needed: the $5 moved QA Alice to Active Chapter 2. Leave it,
  or reverse it via the admin QA panel (/admin/users has gate override controls).
